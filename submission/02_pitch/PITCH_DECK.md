# TRUST//INTERCEPT — Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 — INTERCEPT THE DECISION
**Scams don't need to steal your money first. They need to control your next decision.**
Aegis Signal Lab | HackAI Malaysia 2026 | Track 03

## Slide 2 — The Problem
A scam message can pressure someone to click, pay, reveal an OTP or trust an impersonator before they stop to investigate.

## Slide 3 — Our Insight
Detection asks: **Is this a scam?**
Decision defence asks: **What is it trying to make me do, and what should I safely do next?**

## Slide 4 — How It Works
INTAKE → EVIDENCE → HUNTER / SKEPTIC / VERIFIER → ARBITER → MANIPULATION GRAPH → COUNTERFACTUAL VERIFICATION → HUMAN APPROVAL.

## Slide 5 — Adversarial Evidence Arena
Hunter builds the attack case.
Skeptic searches for benign explanations.
Verifier identifies evidence that could overturn the conclusion.
The system exposes disagreement instead of hiding it.

## Slide 6 — Manipulation Graph
Message → fear/urgency → impersonation → requested action.
The user sees exactly where the decision can be interrupted.

## Slide 7 — Safe Verification
TRUST//INTERCEPT does not tell users to click the suspicious link. It proposes an independent verification channel and records whether evidence supports, contradicts or leaves the claim inconclusive.

## Slide 8 — Evidence Passport + Replay
Every case can be audited: tools, agents, evidence, decision, human approval and integrity manifest.
Replay is read-only and does not re-open suspicious destinations.

## Slide 9 — Scam Immunity
After an incident, the user receives a targeted micro-challenge based on the missed manipulation pattern.
Detect → Learn → Resist the next attempt.

## Slide 10 — Evaluation & Security
Legitimate, borderline and adversarial cases are included to test false positives. Untrusted message content is separated from agent instructions; dangerous network targets and credential-bearing URLs are blocked by policy.

## Slide 11 — Demo
Parcel scam → evidence investigation → adversarial challenge → independent verification → human approval → case replay → immunity challenge.
